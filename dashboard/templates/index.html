Theoretical Takeaways:

  • Floyd-Warshall scales strictly as \(O(V^3)\) (e.g. \(200^3 = 8,000,000\) operations).
  • Bellman-Ford (single-source) scales as \(O(V \cdot E)\) (e.g. \(200 \times 10,148 \approx 2,029,600\) operations).
  • Edmonds-Karp computes maximum flow via BFS augmenting paths in \(O(V \cdot E^2)\).
  • Max-Flow Min-Cut Theorem guarantees that max flow equals total capacity across the bottleneck cut.
